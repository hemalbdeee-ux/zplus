Wayback Downloader downloads publicly archived copies of websites from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine and rebuilds them as files you can host. We are not affiliated with the Internet Archive.

## Your responsibility for content

Owning or buying a domain does not transfer the copyright of the content that was published on it. By starting a restore you confirm that you own the content, have permission to use it, or use it in a way the law allows. Do not use the service to republish other people's work as your own.

## Fair use of the archive

The Internet Archive is a non-profit library. We download slowly and stop when the archive asks us to. Do not try to work around these limits.

## Files and retention

Restored files are kept for 7 days and then deleted. Anyone who has the link to a job can download its files, so share job links with care.

## No guarantee

The archive does not have every page or file of every site. We restore what it has and list what is missing in the report. The service is provided as is.
